🎓 On 11–13 November I taught the students of the Global Campus of Human Rights in Venice (New Technologies cluster): how language models are made and where they fail, open tools built with AI on UN human rights sources and, last but not least, two sessions of Garage Research, in which the students built fantastic apps of their own on UN data. <a href="https://lszoszk.github.io/teaching-materials/global-campus/">The materials</a> are online: the slides, the tools, the readings and what our guests from HURIDOCS and Democracy Reporting International shared in the online session.

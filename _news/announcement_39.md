🎤 Today at the conference "Reframing the Right to Education in International Law? A Focus on Article 13 of the ICESCR" (Track 4), Michał Klichowski and I presented <strong>"Neurotechnology and the Right to Education"</strong>: are limits on the use of such technology, or is it part of high-quality education under Article 13 ICESCR? <a href="https://lszoszk.github.io/talks/neurotechnology-education/">The slides</a> go from Gernsback's 1925 Isolator to EEG headbands in classrooms, and compare the answers of the Council of Europe, UNESCO and the EU AI Act.

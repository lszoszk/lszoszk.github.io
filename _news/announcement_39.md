🎤 At the <a href="https://geneva-academy.ch/event/ahri-conference-2026/">AHRI 2026 conference</a> in Geneva, Michał Klichowski and I spoke on the panel "Reframing the Right to Education in International Law? A Focus on Article 13 of the ICESCR". Our talk, <strong>"Neurotechnology and the Right to Education"</strong>, asked whether limits on the use of such technology are needed, or whether it is part of high-quality education under Article 13 ICESCR. <a href="https://lszoszk.github.io/talks/neurotechnology-education/">The slides</a> go from Gernsback's 1925 Isolator to EEG headbands in classrooms, and compare the approaches of the Council of Europe, UNESCO and the EU AI Act.

🏛️ <a href="https://lszoszk.github.io/travaux-preparatoires/"><strong>UN Travaux Préparatoires</strong></a>, an independent resource built from the UN's own meeting records, is now available in beta: the drafting of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights and the two Covenants (1946–66), speech by speech, with the speaker, delegation, article and the page of the UN record, searchable by words and by meaning, with a Check of the citations in a draft (<a href="https://lszoszk.github.io/tools/travaux/#film">a 90-second film</a> shows how it works).

🎧 New preprint: <a href="https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/jgwpy_v1"><strong>"Binaural-beat hype fails scientific scrutiny again"</strong></a> (Klichowski, Wicher, Szoszkiewicz et al.). In a randomised, single-blind study of <strong>nearly 2,000 adults</strong>, home-use binaural beats did not enhance — and slightly impaired — fluid-intelligence performance, with the sources of interindividual variability still unexplained. The findings indicate that the hype around these consumer neurotechnologies outstrips the evidence.

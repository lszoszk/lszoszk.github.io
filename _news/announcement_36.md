🎬 <a href="https://lszoszk.github.io/ECHR-Dashboard/"><strong>HUDOC Researcher</strong></a>, an independent toolkit built on HUDOC data, is now available in beta: paragraph-level search of the case law of the European Court of Human Rights (over 20,000 judgments in English and over 9,000 only in French), Semantic Search, a citation network and a Check of the citations and quotations in a draft (<a href="https://lszoszk.github.io/tools/hudoc-researcher/#film">a 100-second film</a> shows how it works).

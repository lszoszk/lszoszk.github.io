🎤 On 19 November I gave the opening lecture at the <a href="https://forumpodatkowe.amu.edu.pl/">Forum Podatkowe 2026</a> conference (Faculty of Law and Administration, Adam Mickiewicz University, Poznań; <strong>in Polish</strong>): <a href="https://lszoszk.github.io/talks/ciemna-strona-ksiezyca/"><strong>"Ciemna strona Księżyca"</strong></a> (the dark side of the moon), on how AI changes the work of lawyers. I spoke about 11 Polish rulings that found AI hallucinations in the parties' submissions (4 of them in tax cases), the same test on Article 349 of the Tax Ordinance (which ends at Article 344) for models from successive years and, last but not least, a search tool for the 74 resolutions of the Council for Counteracting Tax Avoidance built with AI in a few minutes (<a href="https://lszoszk.github.io/opinie-rady-puo/">the full version</a> is online). The lecture is in <a href="https://www.youtube.com/live/hegUlw2CP90?t=3110">the recording of the live stream</a> (from 51:50).
